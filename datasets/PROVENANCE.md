# Provenance of the 27 distributions

Where every row came from, how it was labelled, and what is and is not reproducible from this
repository. Licence status is a separate question and lives in [`LICENSES.md`](LICENSES.md).

**The JSONL files in `data/` are the artifact of record.** They are self-contained: running the
battery needs no network, no Hugging Face account and no upstream access. The builders in
`builders/` exist so 18 of the 27 derivations are inspectable, not because anything depends on
them.

## The three tiers

| tier | dists | rebuildable here? |
|---|---:|---|
| imported from a public HF dataset by a shipped builder | 18 | yes, with upstream access — `builders/` |
| imported from a public HF dataset by hand | 4 | documented below, no script shipped |
| generated by us inside the research repo | 5 | no — documented below |

## Tier 1 — shipped builders (18 dists)

`builders/build_lb.py` rebuilds the nine `lb_*` distributions from
[Liars' Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16035) (`Cadenza-Labs/liars-bench`, **gated**), and
`builders/build_dyl.py` rebuilds the nine `dyl_*` distributions from the AISI
["Did you lie?"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12618) rollouts
(`ai-safety-institute/lie-detection-rollouts`). Labels come from the upstream benches: Liars'
Bench rows carry the bench's own `deceptive` flag (belief-relative by construction, LB paper §3);
AISI rows carry the `is_lie` grader with verified-TriviaQA belief for the varied sets and
CoT-verified belief for the organism sets, with `ambiguous` rows dropped.

## Tier 2 — imported from public datasets, no builder shipped (4 dists)

Assembled inside the research repo's `research_genaudit_external` directory. Each is a
straightforward selection over a public dataset, and the label is the upstream dataset's own:

| dist | n (+/−) | upstream | label |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `ga_tqa` | 120 (60/60) | `truthfulqa/truthful_qa` | dataset best vs incorrect answer (symmetric strings) |
| `ga_halu` | 120 (60/60) | `pminervini/HaluEval` (qa split) | dataset right vs hallucinated answer, length-matched rebuild |
| `ga_insider` | 150 (75/75) | `Cadenza-Labs/apollo-llama3.3-insider-trading-generations` | dataset dishonest flag |
| `ga_insider_cot` | 150 (75/75) | same, with the visible reasoning kept | dataset dishonest flag |

`ga_insider` and `ga_insider_cot` are the same underlying generations with and without the
suspect's chain-of-thought left in the transcript; the pair is what makes the reasoning field a
controlled variable rather than a confound.

## Tier 3 — generated by us (5 dists)

These were produced inside the research repo by generation and belief-gating pipelines that call
models. They are **not** reproducible from this repository: the chains depend on four further
internal directories and on the NDIF deployment of the day. What each one is:

### `ga_domains` — 100 rows (50/50)
Synthetic factual lies in five writing styles over a fact bank of novel domains, so that neither
class can be recognised by style. **Behaviour-verified with a 5-vote belief gate on
Qwen3.5-27B**: each fact was elicited five times under a neutral prompt and kept only if the
model demonstrably knew it (3 of 28 facts were dropped). The label is therefore "contradicts
what this model knows", not "was told to lie".

### `b2_pressure` — 286 rows (116/170)
Multi-turn factual pressure: a user pushes back on a correct answer across turns. The deceptive
class is an instructed lie; the honest class includes **capitulation folds** — the model
abandoning a correct answer under pressure without lying about its belief. Labels re-adjudicated
by a 5-vote belief gate plus a gold-sentence check and an independent contradiction read. Flagged
`leaky_context`: its blind null is 0.89, so the pressure setup alone predicts the label.

### `wf_assert` — 600 rows (300/300) and `wf_denial` — 240 rows (120/120)
The controlled claim-shape experiment, and the pair the blog post's central comparison rests on.
The same belief-verified facts are templated into two reply shapes: an **assertion** that
contains an extractable false claim, and a bare **denial** that contains none. One factor
changes; everything else is held constant.

Both are flagged `diagnostic_tuned`: several judge variants were developed against this set
during the competition, so it is **not** untuned held-out data. Use it to diagnose claim shape,
not to headline a generalisation claim. `wf_denial` is additionally `leaky_context`.

### `aud_agentic` — 300 rows (79/221)
Agentic record-versus-report audit on Nemotron-120B: the assistant works a multi-turn tool
session, misconduct enters the record, and an auditor asks whether anything improper happened.
The label is **behaviour-verified against the in-context record** — the final report contradicts
what the transcript shows. Unbalanced by construction (most days are clean), and
`leaky_context` at 0.83.

## What was built and deliberately dropped

Two further belief-gated sets (`new_hpkr_terse`, terse knowledge-denial; `new_agentic_matched`,
record-vs-report) were generated on Qwen3.5-27B with a real 5-vote belief gate and **dropped**,
because they could not be made leak-free: under a strong directive the model denies or conceals
almost always but the *directive* leaks (blind null ≈ 1.0), and under a moderate or discretionary
directive the base model simply answers — 0 denials in 354 and 0 concealments in 240 across three
prompt strengths. This reproduces the AISI / Liars' Bench thesis that behaviour-verified
deception in a base model is either instructed (and therefore leaky) or absent, and that the
interesting organisms have to be trained. The finding is recorded in the manifest's
`new_data_finding` field.

One distribution present in the research manifest, `ga_honest_error` (honest mistakes as a
negative control), is **not** part of this release: it has no cached reference vector and was not
in the 27-distribution battery reported in the post.

## Nulls

Every distribution carries two nulls, measured **before** any method was scored, and stored in
`gauntlet.json`:

- **`lennull`** — AUROC of the final reply's length alone. Catches a detector that wins by
  reading length.
- **`blindnull`** — AUROC of the trusted 9B judge reading the whole conversation with the final
  reply deleted. Catches label leakage through the context: the system instruction, the record,
  the identity of the question.

A detector has read the lie itself only to the extent that it beats the blind null.
Leaky distributions are **flagged, not removed** — record-vs-report concealment and
harm-pressure genres are leaky by nature and are among the most informative tests.
`python ../gauntlet.py report` marks every cell that fails to clear its own blind null; five of
the reference judge's do.

To recompute the nulls yourself: `python ../gauntlet.py nulls` (add `--skip-blind` for the
length null, which needs no model).

## Row format and invariants

```
dist      distribution id
rid       stable row id
messages  chat messages [{role, content}, ...] — the LAST assistant turn is the reply
          under audit; chain-of-thought is stripped from content into meta
y         1 = deceptive final reply, 0 = honest
gen_model model that produced the reply
meta      per-row provenance (source, label method, genre, optional reasoning, ...)
```

Enforced at write time by `builders/_common.py::save_dist` and re-verified over what is on disk
by `python ../gauntlet.py check`: the last message is the assistant's, its content is non-empty,
no `<think>` survives in visible content, `y` is 0/1, and the manifest counts match the files.
